Notes from building a seating planner

These are the questions that keep showing up while someone is still in a spreadsheet: how the chart actually gets made, how many chairs a round table takes, which card goes where, and how a tent-card sheet is laid out.

  • How to make a wedding seating chart

    Import the list first. Group families before you touch a table. Lock the top table, then the people who must not sit together. Print the chart and the cards from that one plan so they cannot disagree.

  • How many people fit at a 60-inch round table?

    Eight comfortably, ten if you squeeze. Circumference divided by 24 inches of elbow room, a table of every common diameter, and how much floor to leave between tables.

  • Escort cards vs place cards

    One sends a guest to a table. The other sends them to a chair. Most rooms need both, and the usual failure is typing the two lists separately so someone ends up at two tables.

  • Avery 5302 place card template

    3.5 × 2 in folded, four tent cards per Letter sheet, 0.75 in side margins, and the print setting that ruins a packet.
